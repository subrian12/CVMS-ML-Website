1How to Register

Registration consists of three components. Please complete all steps before the deadline.

1.1Registration Form

Complete the registration form to sign up for Math League. Make sure all information is accurate before submitting.

1.2Signature Form

Both the student and parent must sign the signature form. Place the signed form in an envelope and turn it in to the Math League box in the CVMS front office as soon as possible, or before our first class.

1.3Suggested Donation

A suggested donation of $50. You can donate either by check or online.
By check: make it out to CVMS PTSA with a note to Math League and your student's full name. Include the check in the same envelope as your signature form.
Online: donate through the CVMS PTSA donation page.
