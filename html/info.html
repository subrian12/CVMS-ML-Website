General
Information

Time and Date:

Our math league meets weekly in person on Wednesday evenings from 6:30 pm to 8 pm. We will not have class during summer and other school breaks.

Location:

We will be using rooms 601 (... class), 603 (... class), and 604 (... class).

Dropoff and Pickup:

The only gate for the students to leave in the evenings at 8pm will be the gate by the gym (in the corner) by the lunch lines. All M.L. students will leave from that gate, and parents can wait for their students at the Administration parking lot. We cannot allow parents/siblings on campus at any time during the hours from 6:30pm—8:00pm to wait for their students.
