01 - GENERAL

General Information

All the logistics behind our weekly classes — where and when we meet, how dropoff and pickup works, and how to reach us if you have any questions.

Time and Date:

Our math league meets weekly in person on Wednesday evenings from 6:30 pm to 8:00 pm. We will not have class during summer and other major school breaks. If there are any changes to time or date, they will be sent out through email.

Location:

We will be using rooms 601 (Factorial class), 603 (Infinity class), 605 (Power class), and 604 (Integral class) on the CVMS campus. Please refer to the two maps above.

Dropoff and Pickup:

The only gate for students to leave at 8pm is the gate by the gym. Parents can wait at the Administration parking lot. No parents or siblings on campus during 6:30–8pm.

Contact / Questions:

Email us at abcd@gmail.com or ask during class. If you missed the placement test and are joining late, please reach out as well.

02 - CLASS

Class Information

More details about the class placement test, our regular class structure, and the classes we offer to accomodate for all students.

Class Placement:

Our amazing group of coaches will conduct a placement test in order to find out the right level of challenge for our students and diagnose each student's individual ability. The exam will be in-person during the first meeting on Wednesday, September 3, 2025. Students must bring their Chromebooks, as they may have to use them to submit their exams. If you are unable to take the exam, please email us. After several classes, coaches may decide to move certain students to classes that suit them better.

Class Program:

Most classes, our coaches will deliver a short lecture on a new topic followed by problem-solving practice. The lectures will cover a wide range of topics, which can be found below. The problem-solving sessions will allow students to apply what they've learned and develop their critical thinking skills. Other classes, we will have friendly class competitions to prepare students for real contests.

01
Factorial

Factorial is our beginner-friendly class, and no prior competition experience is required. Coaches guide students through core fundamentals like basic algebra and logical thinking, while gradually introducing them to the problem-solving mindset that makes math exciting.

02
Power

Power is ideal for students who excel in school math and are curious about what lies beyond the standard curriculum. This class bridges the gap between classroom math and competition math, introducing students to creative problem-solving strategies and mathematical reasoning.

03
Integral

Integral is designed for students who have some exposure to competitive math and are ready to take the next step. Topics include intermediate algebra, counting and probability, and basic geometry. Students who have taken the AMC 8 or are preparing for the AMC 10 will find this class to be the perfect challenge.

04
Infinity

Infinity is our most advanced class. Students dive deep into topics such as advanced algebra, Euclidean geometry, combinatorics, and number theory. Students are expected to have a solid foundation in competitive math and the drive to tackle challenging problems.
