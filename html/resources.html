02 - COMPETITIONS

Competitions

A guide to the competitions our students participate in throughout the year — what to expect, when they are, and who can compete.

AMC
10A
AMC 10A
A 25-question, 75-minute multiple choice exam for students in grade 10 or below. Covers algebra, geometry, number theory, and combinatorics. Top scorers qualify for the AIME.
Nov 5, 2026 25 questions 75 minutes Grade 10 and below
AMC
10B
AMC 10B
An alternate sitting of the AMC 10 with a different problem set. Students may take both sittings. Top scorers qualify for the AIME.
Nov 12, 2026 25 questions 75 minutes Grade 10 and below
AMC
8
AMC 8
A 25-question, 40-minute competition for students in grade 8 or below. A great entry point into competitive math covering arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and logical reasoning.
Jan 25, 2027 25 questions 40 minutes Grade 8 and below
MC
CHAP
Mathcounts Chapter
The regional qualifying round of Mathcounts. Features Sprint, Target, and Team rounds. Top performers advance to the state competition.
Mar 7, 2027 Sprint, Target, Team Grade 8 and below
MC
STATE
Mathcounts State
The state-level Mathcounts competition for top scorers from chapter. The most prestigious event on our calendar — students who advance represent our region and compete for a spot at nationals.
Apr 10, 2027 Sprint, Target, Team, Countdown Top chapter scorers only
MC
NATL
Mathcounts National
The national Mathcounts competition held annually in Washington D.C. The most elite level of middle school math competition in the country — only the top state scorers advance. Features Sprint, Target, Team, and Countdown rounds.
May 11, 2027 Sprint, Target, Team, Countdown Top state scorers only Washington D.C.
