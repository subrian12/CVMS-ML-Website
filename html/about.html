About Us

Time/Date Class Information History
Our mission is to provide the needed friendly environment for students to explore mathematics together under the guidance of our experienced coaches

Time/Date

Time. These groups meet in person on Wednesday evenings from 6:30 pm to 8 pm.
Location. We will be using rooms 601, 603, and 604.
Pick up. The only gate for the students to leave in the evenings at 8pm will be the gate by the gym (in the corner) by the lunch lines. All M.L. students will leave from that gate, and parents can wait for their students at the Administration parking lot. We cannot allow parents/siblings on campus at any time during the hours from 6:30pm—8:00pm to wait for their students.

Class Information

Classes. Our amazing group of coaches will conduct a placement test in order to find out the right level of challenge for our students. Accordingly, we will have three groups called infinity, power, and factorial. Placement test. Our coaches will give a placement test to find the right level of challenge for our students. The exam will be in-person during the first meeting on Wednesday, September 3, 2025. Students must bring their Chromebooks, as they may have to use them to submit their exams.
