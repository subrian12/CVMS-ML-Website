01 - BECOME A VOLUNTEER

Become a Volunteer

Parent volunteers are essential to our program — from weekly supervision to proctoring competitions throughout the year.

Weekly Commitment

Each Wednesday evening requires at least one parent volunteer per classroom — a minimum of four volunteers every week. Volunteers help supervise students and support coaches during sessions.

Competition Proctoring

Throughout the year we need volunteers to help proctor competitions including the AMC 8, AMC 10, and Mathcounts. Signup forms for each event will be posted here when available.

Requirements

All volunteers must be cleared through the district before volunteering on campus. Please complete this before classes begin.

1
Create an account and fill out the EdJoin online form
2
Complete this additional form and email it to Ms. Roberta Blank at roberta.blank@sduhsd.net
3
Sign up here to volunteer for a classroom session once cleared

Reimbursement

PTSA (Math League) can reimburse your expenses for the clearance process. Reach out to us at abcd@gmail.com for more details.

02 - DONATIONS

Donations

Your generosity directly supports our students and keeps our program accessible to everyone.

Why We Need Your Support

Running a competitive math program involves real costs. Competition registration fees, printed materials, classroom supplies, and event costs all add up over the course of the year. Your donations help ensure that every student — regardless of financial situation — can fully participate in everything Math League has to offer.

Where Your Money Goes

Donations go directly toward AMC and Mathcounts registration fees, printed handouts and worksheets for all four class levels, supplies for our end-of-year closing party and ceremony, and any additional materials our coaches need throughout the year.

How to Donate

Every dollar goes directly toward supporting our students. No donation is too small — we are deeply grateful for any support you can offer.
