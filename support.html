1Become a Volunteer

Parent volunteers are essential to our program — from weekly supervision to proctoring competitions throughout the year.

1.1Weekly Commitment

Each Wednesday evening requires at least one parent volunteer per classroom — a minimum of four volunteers every week. Volunteers do not play an active role during class, they are there for adult supervision.

1.2Competition Proctoring

Throughout the year we need volunteers to help proctor competitions including the AMC 8 and AMC 10.

1.3Volunteer Form

If you are interested in volunteering for math league, please fill out this google form.

2Donations

Your generosity directly supports our students and keeps our program accessible to everyone.

2.1Why We Need Your Support

Running a competitive math program involves real costs. Competition registration fees, printed materials, classroom supplies, and event costs all add up over the course of the year. Your donations help ensure that every student — regardless of financial situation — can fully participate in everything Math League has to offer.

2.2Where Your Money Goes

Donations go directly toward AMC and Mathcounts registration fees, printed handouts and worksheets for all four class levels, supplies for our end-of-year closing party and ceremony, and any additional materials our coaches need throughout the year.

2.3How to Donate

Every dollar goes directly toward supporting our students. No donation is too small — we are deeply grateful for any support you can offer. You can donate by check (see the registration page for details) or online through the CVMS PTSA.
