A Note from CVMS Administration

Hi math league! We are glad to welcome you after school. Please follow the expectations below:

  • Please arrive at 6:30pm. If your parent needs to drop you off early, please wait in the front of the school.
  • No bikes or scooters on campus, they need to be parked in the front on bike racks.
  • No food in the classrooms, and please pick up your trash outside.
  • Please don't touch any of the teacher's belongings in their classroom.
  • Please walk on campus and utilize appropriate volume when moving around campus.

Thank you!

— CVMS Administration
